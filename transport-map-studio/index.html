Transport Map Studio — Universal Bend Geometry Tester

True parallel offsets · circular fillets · configurable direction, turn angle, frequency and radius

All paths are constructed as exact offsets from one rounded centreline. A turn with insufficient inner radius is rejected rather than distorted. For turns over 90°, longer approach and exit straights are automatically provided.

Direction angles are measured clockwise from east (screen coordinates). Spacing is centre-to-centre, perpendicular to the local tangent. The bend is a circular arc, not a quadratic Bézier approximation.
